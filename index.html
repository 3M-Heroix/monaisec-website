About MONAISEC

Research at the intersection of finance, AI and cybersecurity.

MONAISEC is an independent research initiative focused on understanding the security, reliability, governance and systemic implications of autonomous AI agents interacting with financial and monetary systems.

Our aim is to develop rigorous analysis, practical risk perspectives and accessible intelligence for researchers, financial institutions, technology teams and policymakers.

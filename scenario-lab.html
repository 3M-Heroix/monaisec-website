METHODOLOGY & LIMITATIONS

Explore pathways, not predictions.

Each scenario is a constructed analytical exercise assembled from the threat taxonomy, financial risk register and regulatory intelligence database. Linked regulations are prompts for jurisdiction-specific review—not legal determinations.

Stages and warning signals are illustrative. The lab does not calculate likelihood, severity, expected loss or systemic impact. Real outcomes depend on architecture, autonomy, permissions, transaction scale, market conditions, institutional controls and supervisory context.

For research, tabletop exercise and risk-discussion purposes only. Not investment, legal, supervisory or operational advice.

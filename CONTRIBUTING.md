# Contributing to MONAISEC research

1. Use stable IDs; never recycle an ID after deletion.
2. Prefer official primary sources and link directly to the authoritative text.
3. Record publication, effective and last-verified dates separately where applicable. Use ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`.
4. Separate binding law, supervisory guidance, voluntary frameworks, technical standards, research and policy statements.
5. Distinguish observed incidents, demonstrated attacks, known vulnerabilities, threat patterns and analytical scenarios.
6. Label MONAISEC interpretations and graph mappings; do not attribute an analytical conclusion to a source unless it explicitly says so.
7. Include caveats, affected scope, jurisdiction and verification notes.
8. Run `python scripts/validate_data.py` before submitting changes.
9. Update `CHANGELOG.md` for material additions or corrections.
10. Do not describe the seed datasets as exhaustive or the qualitative assessments as calibrated quantitative risk scores.

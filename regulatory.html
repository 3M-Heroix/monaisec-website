How to read this register

Legal force describes the instrument's legal character; agent relevance describes how directly it addresses agentic systems. These are separate dimensions. We do not assign an arbitrary jurisdiction-wide strictness score. A framework may be highly relevant to agents without being legally binding. Records are research summaries, not legal advice. Always consult the official text and competent authority.

Methodology, scope & limitations

This is a curated initial analytical register, not a quantitative risk assessment, stress test, incident database or legal opinion. It distinguishes (1) vulnerabilities explicitly identified in official publications, (2) established financial transmission channels, and (3) MONAISEC agent-specific analytical pathways. The presence of an official source does not mean that the source validates every agent scenario in the record. Consequences are conditional and depend on deployment context, autonomy, permissions, scale, market conditions, provider concentration, substitutability and controls. Related regulatory IDs are navigation cross-references, not legal determinations of applicability.

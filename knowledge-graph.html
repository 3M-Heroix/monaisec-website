Relationship methodology

Threat → regulation: analytical cross-reference indicating a potentially relevant instrument to assess; it is not a legal conclusion or proof of direct coverage. Threat → sector: potential exposure pathway, not evidence of an observed incident. Threat → control: mitigation themes grouped from the current taxonomy; they do not certify control effectiveness. All relationships are generated from the MONAISEC seed datasets and should be reviewed as those datasets expand.

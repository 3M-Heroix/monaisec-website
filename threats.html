Methodology & evidence discipline

This is a curated initial taxonomy, not a complete vulnerability catalogue or a list of confirmed financial-sector incidents. OWASP and MITRE mappings provide a shared vocabulary; they do not prove that a specific attack has occurred in a financial institution. Financial examples describe plausible exposure pathways unless explicitly labelled as observed evidence. No universal severity score is assigned: impact depends on autonomy, permissions, system criticality, transaction value, exposure and controls. Consult the linked primary frameworks and relevant regulatory records before operational use. Further official guidance: Joint CISA/NSA/ASD/CCCS/NCSC guidance on careful adoption of agentic AI ↗ · NIST AI Agent Standards Initiative ↗.

    03 / RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

    Traceable by design.

    01 · Evidence hierarchy

    Primary legislation and official regulator sources first; official supervisory materials and standard-setters next; research and security frameworks are contextual.

    02 · Legal force is explicit

    Binding law, supervisory guidance, voluntary standards and proposals are separate categories. They are never collapsed into a single score.

    03 · Conditions are visible

    Every rule carries scope conditions, role assumptions, source links and evidence prompts. “Potentially applicable” is not a legal conclusion.

    04 · Version-aware updates

    Official-source monitoring creates review candidates. Human verification is required before a source or obligation is marked current.
